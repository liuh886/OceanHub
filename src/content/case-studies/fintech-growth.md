## The Challenge

NeoBank Corp was experiencing hyper-growth, onboarding 50,000 new users per month. However, their legacy monolithic architecture was struggling to scale. Downtime incidents were increasing, and the support team was overwhelmed. With an IPO planned in 12 months, stability was non-negotiable.

## Our Solution

NBW deployed a cross-functional team to address the scalability issues on two fronts:

1.  **Technical Refactoring:** We migrated the core banking ledger to a microservices architecture on AWS, implementing auto-scaling groups and a multi-region database strategy.
2.  **Operational Efficiency:** We integrated an AI-powered triage system for customer support, capable of resolving 60% of common queries (password resets, transaction status) without human intervention.

## The Results

*   **Uptime:** Improved from 98.5% to 99.99% during peak transaction volumes.
*   **Cost Savings:** Cloud spend reduced by 20% through efficient resource scheduling.
*   **Customer Satisfaction:** NPS score rose from 45 to 72 within 6 months.
*   **IPO Success:** The company successfully listed on NASDAQ in Q4 2024.
